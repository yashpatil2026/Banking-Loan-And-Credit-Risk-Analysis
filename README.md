🏦 Banking Loan & Credit Risk Analysis

📌 About the Project

A beginner-friendly Power BI project to analyze banking loans, customer credit profiles, and payment performance.

🛠️ Tools Used

- Power BI
- Power Query
- DAX

📂 Dataset

The project contains three tables:

- Customers: Customer details, income, and credit scores.
- Loans: Loan amounts, types, and loan status.
- Payments: Payment status, due amounts, and paid amounts.

Helps identify loan categories with higher loan volumes.
3. Monthly Loan Trends
Tracks the number of loans across all 12 months.
Shows fluctuations in monthly loan activity.
Helps identify months with relatively high or low loan counts.
4. Loan Amount by Type
Compares the total loan amount across different loan categories.
Home Loans contribute the largest amount in the displayed comparison.
Credit Card loans contribute the smallest amount in the displayed comparison.
5. Loan Status Analysis
Displays the distribution of Active, Closed, Late Payment, and Defaulted loans.
Helps identify loans that may require additional monitoring.
💡 Key Insights
The dashboard reports 1,200 total loans and a total loan amount of approximately 2 billion.
Home Loans represent the largest loan amount among the displayed categories.
Monthly loan counts fluctuate throughout the year.
Defaulted and late-payment loans should be monitored to understand credit risk.
🎯 Business Objective
To monitor loan portfolio performance, understand loan distribution, and identify potential credit risks.

🔍 Analysis
This dashboard analyzes customer distribution, credit score categories, and customer demographics.
1. Customer Distribution by City
Compares the number of customers across cities such as Thane, Nashik, Ahmedabad, Mumbai, Chennai, Nagpur, Delhi, Hyderabad, Pune, and Bengaluru.
Thane has the highest customer count among the cities displayed.
Bengaluru has the lowest customer count among the cities displayed.
2. Customer Distribution by Credit Score Band
Categorizes customers into Excellent, Fair, Good, and Poor credit score bands.
Excellent, Fair, and Good categories have relatively similar customer counts.
The Poor credit score category has noticeably fewer customers than the other displayed categories.
3. Customer Distribution by Gender
Shows the distribution of customers across Other, Female, and Male categories.
Helps compare customer representation across gender groups.
4. Interactive City Filter
Allows users to select individual cities.
Helps analyze customer profiles for a specific location.
5. Employment Type Filter
Provides filtering by Business Owner, Contract, Salaried, Self-employed, and Unemployed.
Helps explore customer profiles by employment category.
💡 Key Insights
Thane has the highest customer count in the displayed city comparison.
The Excellent, Fair, and Good credit score groups have similar customer counts.
The Poor credit score group is comparatively smaller.
City and employment filters make it easier to investigate customer segments.
🎯 Business Objective
To understand customer demographics and credit score distributions, helping the bank evaluate customer segments and support informed lending decisions.

🔍 Analysis
This dashboard focuses on monthly payment activity, payment status distribution, and outstanding due amounts.
1. Monthly Payments Trend
Tracks monthly payment activity from January to December.
Payment activity generally increases from February through August.
August shows the highest point in the displayed trend.
Payment activity drops sharply in November before showing a slight increase in December.
2. Payment Status Distribution
Categorizes payment records into Paid, Late, Missed, and Pending.
Paid payments form the largest segment in the displayed chart.
Late, Missed, and Pending payments represent other payment outcomes that require monitoring.
3. Outstanding Due Amount by Payment Status
Compares the sum of due amounts for Paid, Late, Pending, and Missed payment categories.
The Paid category has the largest displayed due-amount total.
The Late, Pending, and Missed categories have smaller displayed totals.
4. Loan Type Comparison
Displays loan counts by loan type.
Helps compare payment-related data across the different loan categories when used with other dashboard filters.
💡 Key Insights
Monthly payment activity reaches its highest displayed point around August.
Payment activity declines sharply in November.
Paid payments represent the largest segment in the payment status chart.
Late, Missed, and Pending payments should be monitored to identify potential repayment issues.
Outstanding due amounts can help the bank prioritize further payment investigation.
🎯 Business Objective
To monitor payment performance, understand monthly payment trends, and identify repayment categories that may require additional attention.

💡 Key Insights

- Analyze loan distribution by loan type.
- Track active and defaulted loans.
- Understand customer credit score categories.
- Monitor monthly payment trends.
- Identify late payments and outstanding amounts.

To understand loan portfolio performance and credit risk through interactive dashboards.
